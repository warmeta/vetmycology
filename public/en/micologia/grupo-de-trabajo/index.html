Research Group

Veterinary Mycology Group · ULPGC

The Veterinary Mycology Group of the Universidad de Las Palmas de Gran Canaria is made up of the following members:

  • Dr. Begoña Acosta Hernández — Principal Investigator
  • Dr. Inmaculada Rosario Medina
  • Dr. Soraya Déniz Suárez
  • Dr. Otilia Ferrer Quintana

The group carries out its research in the field of veterinary mycology, with particular focus on mycoses in domestic and wild animals of the Canary Islands.
