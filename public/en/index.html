VETERINARY MYCOLOGY GROUP

Faculty of Veterinary Medicine · Universidad de Las Palmas de Gran Canaria

We study fungal diseases in domestic animals, reptiles, and fish. Our work integrates clinical mycological diagnosis with research into the epidemiology, pathogenesis, and treatment of the main veterinary mycoses.

The group is led by Dr. Begoña Acosta Hernández and is part of the University Institute for Animal Health and Food Safety (IUSA) at ULPGC.

Mycology

Biology and general characteristics of fungal pathogens of veterinary interest.

Pathologies

Candidiasis, cryptococcosis, aspergillosis and other mycoses in domestic and wild animals.

Laboratory

Mycological diagnostic protocols and techniques used in the group's laboratory.

Research

Scientific publications, conference communications, and reference articles.
